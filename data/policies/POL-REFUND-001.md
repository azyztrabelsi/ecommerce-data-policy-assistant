# Refund Policy

> SIMULATED capstone content. This is not an actual Olist policy.

## 1. Purpose and scope
This policy explains when a customer can receive a refund for an order and how the refund is paid.

## 2. Eligibility
2.1 A delivered item is refundable if it is returned within the return window of POL-RETURN-001 and in its original condition.
2.2 An order is refundable in full if it has not been delivered more than 10 days after its estimated delivery date.
2.3 An order with status "unavailable" is refunded in full automatically.
2.4 A duplicate payment for the same order is refunded in full after verification.

## 3. Exclusions
3.1 Perishable items (food and drink) cannot be refunded after delivery unless they arrive damaged.
3.2 Items damaged by the customer are not refundable.
3.3 Requests made after the return window are not refundable, except for defective or wrong items (see POL-RETURN-001).

## 4. Evidence
4.1 The customer must give the order ID.
4.2 For damaged, defective or wrong items, photos must be sent within 7 days of delivery.

## 5. Refund amount
5.1 The refund covers the item price.
5.2 The freight value is refunded when the error is the seller's, or when the order was delivered more than 7 days after the estimated delivery date (see POL-DELIVERY-001).
5.3 Otherwise, freight is not refunded.

## 6. Refund method and time
6.1 Refunds are paid with the original payment method.
6.2 Processing time: credit card 7 business days, debit card 5 business days, boleto 10 business days (bank transfer), voucher 2 business days (as voucher credit).

## 7. Escalation
7.1 If a refund is not received after the times in section 6, the agent escalates to a supervisor, who answers within 2 business days.
