# Cancellation Policy

> SIMULATED capstone content. This is not an actual Olist policy.

## 1. When cancellation is allowed
1.1 An order can be canceled for free while its status is created, approved, invoiced or processing.
1.2 An order with status shipped cannot be canceled. The customer must wait for delivery and use POL-RETURN-001.
1.3 An order with status delivered cannot be canceled; use the return policy.

## 2. How to request a cancellation
2.1 The customer contacts support with the order ID.
2.2 Support confirms the cancellation within 1 business day.

## 3. Cancellations by the seller or system
3.1 If the item is out of stock, the order gets the status unavailable and is refunded automatically.
3.2 Orders with an unpaid boleto after 3 days are canceled automatically (see POL-PAYMENT-001).

## 4. Refunds after cancellation
4.1 If payment was already made, the refund follows POL-REFUND-001, section 6.
4.2 If no payment was made, nothing is charged.
