# Payment Policy

> SIMULATED capstone content. This is not an actual Olist policy.

## 1. Accepted payment methods
1.1 credit_card, debit_card, boleto and voucher.

## 2. Installments
2.1 Credit card payments can be split into up to 24 installments.
2.2 Interest may apply, so the total paid can be higher than the item price plus freight.
2.3 The minimum installment value is 5 BRL.

## 3. Boleto
3.1 A boleto is valid for 3 days.
3.2 An unpaid boleto cancels the order automatically.

## 4. Vouchers
4.1 A voucher can be combined with another payment method.
4.2 Refunds for voucher payments are returned as voucher credit.

## 5. Failed payments
5.1 A customer can retry a failed payment up to 3 times.
5.2 The order is only created after the payment is approved.

## 6. Duplicate payments
6.1 If a customer was charged twice for the same order, the agent verifies both payments.
6.2 The duplicate amount is refunded under POL-REFUND-001, section 6.
