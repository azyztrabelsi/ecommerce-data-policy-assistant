# Customer Support FAQ

> SIMULATED capstone content. This is not an actual Olist policy.

## 1. How long does a refund take?
1.1 Between 2 and 10 business days depending on the payment method. See POL-REFUND-001, section 6.

## 2. What does the status "unavailable" mean?
2.1 The item is out of stock and the order cannot be fulfilled. The customer is refunded automatically.

## 3. What can I do if my order is late?
3.1 Tracking information is shared first. If the delay passes 5 days after the estimated date, support opens a carrier investigation. See POL-DELIVERY-001, section 3.

## 4. What if I received a wrong or defective item?
4.1 The customer has 30 days after delivery. Photos must be sent within 7 days. See POL-RETURN-001.

## 5. Can I change the delivery address after ordering?
5.1 No. The order must be canceled while it is still created, approved, invoiced or processing, and then placed again.

## 6. Who can help if the agent cannot resolve a case?
6.1 The agent escalates to a supervisor, who answers within 2 business days.

## 7. Are these policies real?
7.1 No. All policies in this pack are simulated for a capstone project.
