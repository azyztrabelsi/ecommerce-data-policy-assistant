# Delivery Policy

> SIMULATED capstone content. This is not an actual Olist policy.

## 1. Estimated delivery date
1.1 The estimated delivery date is shown when the order is placed.
1.2 A delivery is late when the delivered date is after the estimated delivery date.

## 2. Order statuses
2.1 created: order registered. approved: payment confirmed. invoiced: invoice issued. processing: seller is preparing the item.
2.2 shipped: handed to the carrier. delivered: received by the customer.
2.3 canceled: order canceled. unavailable: the item is out of stock and the order cannot be fulfilled.

## 3. Delays and support actions
3.1 Up to 5 days after the estimated date with no delivery: the agent shares tracking information, no other action.
3.2 More than 5 days after the estimated date with no delivery: the agent opens a carrier investigation.
3.3 More than 15 days after the estimated date with no delivery: the order is treated as lost, and the customer chooses a reshipment or a full refund.

## 4. Compensation for late delivery
4.1 If an order is delivered more than 7 days after the estimated date, the freight value is refunded (see POL-REFUND-001, section 5).
4.2 Deliveries up to 7 days late receive no compensation.

## 5. Failed delivery
5.1 The carrier makes 2 delivery attempts.
5.2 After 2 failed attempts the order returns to the seller and is handled as a cancellation.
